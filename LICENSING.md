# Licensing

A plain summary of how plymeld is licensed. The licenses themselves are what count: [LICENSE](LICENSE) (the GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3) and [NOTICE](NOTICE). This page is not legal advice.

only** (SPDX: `AGPL-3.0-only`). You may use it, study it, change it, and share it, with or without changes, under that
license's terms. In short: if you share plymeld or a modified version, or let people use a modified version over a
network, you offer them its complete source under the same license.

## What plymeld makes is yours

The g-code, plan files, pictures, and other files plymeld writes from your models and settings are not covered by the
AGPL; use and share them however you like. Where plymeld copies some of its own material into them (bundled settings
values, templates, comments), [NOTICE](NOTICE) grants an additional permission to use it without restriction.

## Building on plymeld needs nothing signed

Forking plymeld, using it in research or a paper, trying an idea in a copy of it, or shipping a modified version under
the AGPL: none of these needs any agreement with Russell Tech Strategy LLC. The AGPL is the whole permission.

## A commercial license, by agreement

To modify plymeld or build it into a product **without** publishing your source under the AGPL, Russell Tech Strategy
LLC offers a separate commercial license by agreement: [legal@plymeld.org](mailto:legal@plymeld.org).

## Contributing: the contributor license agreement (CLA)

Because plymeld is offered under both licenses, a contribution merged into this repository comes under a CLA. You keep
the copyright in your contribution and grant Russell Tech Strategy LLC a license to it, including the right to license
it on other terms, such as the commercial license. A bot asks you to accept the [individual CLA](legal/individual-cla.md)
once, on your first pull request; a company contributing on its employees' behalf signs the
[corporate CLA](legal/corporate-cla.md). [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) has the details.

The CLA is needed only to have a contribution merged here. It is not needed to use, fork, or build on plymeld.
