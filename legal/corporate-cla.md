**Parties.** This Agreement is between the organization named below (the "Corporation") and
Russell Tech Strategy LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company (the "Company"), which owns and
runs the plymeld project.

**Why it exists.** plymeld is published under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3,
and the Company also offers it under commercial licenses. To do both with code that others
contribute, the Company needs written permission from whoever owns that code. This Agreement lets
the Corporation contribute work it owns, authorize named employees to contribute on its behalf,
and grant the licenses below. The Corporation keeps ownership of its Contributions and remains
free to use them in any other way.

**How to accept.** Complete and sign this Agreement and email it to
legal@plymeld.org. Once signed, it covers every Contribution the Corporation makes,

**"The Corporation"** includes every entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under

**"Project"** means plymeld and any other software project the Company owns or manages.

**"Contribution"** means the works identified in Schedule B, if any, and any work of authorship

representatives in any form, including a pull request, patch, issue, comment, or message in the
